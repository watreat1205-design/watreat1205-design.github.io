Privacy Policy for VisionComfort

VisionComfort: Eye Care is an Android application designed to help users explore comfortable reading settings, monitor reading-comfort preferences, and develop healthier screen habits.

We respect your privacy and aim to keep your personal information under your control.

1. Information Stored on Your Device

VisionComfort does not require account registration. Information such as Vision Comfort Check results, reading preferences, progress history, Daily Reset activity, and reminder settings is stored locally on your device.

The app does not upload these personal reading-comfort records to developer-operated servers.

2. Device Permissions

VisionComfort may request access to certain device features to provide its functionality:

  • Display settings: To apply user-selected reading-comfort settings.
  • Notifications: To provide reminders and screen-break notifications.
  • Usage access: To support screen-time monitoring features when enabled by the user.
  • Internet and network access: To load external websites in Web Reader and support internet-dependent functionality.

3. Web Reader

The Web Reader allows users to open external websites using adjustable reading settings.

These websites may collect information, use cookies, or apply their own privacy policies. VisionComfort does not control the privacy practices of external websites.

4. Advertising and Third-Party Services

VisionComfort does not display third-party advertisements and no longer uses Google AdMob to serve advertisements in its current release.

Some published versions may contain the Meta (Facebook) SDK, previously integrated to measure advertising campaign effectiveness. Depending on the SDK configuration, Meta may automatically process app events and technical device information.

For more information about Meta's data practices, visit Meta Privacy Policy .

5. Data Storage and Deletion

VisionComfort stores reading preferences, check history, and related app data locally on your device.

Users can clear locally stored app data through Android's application settings. Uninstalling the app normally removes its local data, subject to applicable Android or device transfer and backup functionality.

6. External Resources

VisionComfort may provide educational information and links to external resources. Third-party websites are responsible for their own content and privacy practices.

7. Health Information Disclaimer

VisionComfort is intended for informational and general reading-comfort purposes only. It does not diagnose medical conditions, provide medical treatment, or replace professional eye examinations.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in the application's features, third-party services, or data practices.

9. Contact

For questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact: visioncomfort.app@gmail.com .
